All five cases; use only recorded top ten proposal band polygons and scores. Original results and target geometry are already known. No actual native score maps/masks are available in this environment. This measures full-chart target-box geometric coverage only, NOT masked coverage or review recall. In particular the matched control is sparse support; do not treat unobserved area as valid.

Baseline: each of the first two proposals in existing score order gets a 20mm square centered at its polygon centroid, translated to stay inside chart. If fewer proposals, choose that many; zero proposals means zero crops, not a negative verdict. If either chart dimension <20mm, do not construct a window (no proposed case is expected to have this problem).

Method: on a 0.25mm physical grid at half-cell centers, assign each sample the maximum positive score of any of the ten archived band polygons containing it, or zero. Candidate squares are 20mm per side, with start positions on a 2mm lattice plus final edge-aligned positions and clamped baseline positions. Greedily choose up to two squares with maximum additional uncovered score mass, using lexicographic top-left (y,x) tie break, no repeat; keep same number as baseline. Use only chart shape, pitch and proposal metadata, no target annotations. A maximum over proposals prevents overlapping detections from contributing multiple votes. A zero-gain second window is still selected at fixed budget, but disclosed.

After selection, calculate exact continuous geometric coverage of each source target box by the union of the two rectangles, and coverage of its horizontal centerline by their union. Record union chart area and individual windows. 306B: minimum of two row-centerline fractions and average; 343: number of 18 source boxes with ≥50% geometric overlap, mean box fraction. The blank and shuffle proposal lists are process controls, not FPR estimates. Separate full and matched 306B results; don't pool them or claim actual valid-pixel recall.

Gate for requesting fresh native-mask/reader validation: full-support 306B minimum row-centerline fraction improves ≥10 percentage points, neither 306B row loses >5 points, 343 count does not decrease, 343 mean box coverage not >5 points worse, no extra review windows or bigger crop; both zero-proposal cases stay empty. Report every gate and all results, no tuning on these data. If it fails, close this placement intervention and retain baseline/full-field review. CPU only, no GPU or external data.
