# Adaptive 044 — Q12 single-transverse-slice polar winding test (Claude 020 item 4)

2026-11-23. Distinct from winding_audit's storage-invariant track-chain fix; does not require new track stores or other agents' compute. Test physical single-height signed radial drift without axial taper. Source coarse eligible CT, at level 1, no ink maps or model calls. Fixed native z at midpoint of Claude S4 fitted bands: PHerc0175A 7650 (catalog ACW control), PHerc1203 9700, PHerc0846A 3100. Volumes: 20250521115057-8.640um-1.2m-116keV-masked.zarr; 20250820131727-9.362um-1.2m-113keV-masked.zarr; 20250728152254-9.362um-1.2m-113keV-masked.zarr. Level 1 pitch 17.28 or 18.724 µm. Acquisition: one transverse CT slice per scroll, range-read exactly its 2D planes in uncompressed C-order uint8 Zarr v2 chunks. Verify 206, Content-Range, body length; no whole-volume download; compressed/unsupported metadata fail rather than silently expand budget. 404 absent stored chunk -> unavailable mask, never measured background. Cap 160 MiB actual charged bodies / 4500 requests total, 12 parallel HTTP workers, no retry, 6-minute CPU workflow. Record slice hashes, source metadata hashes, availability and request bytes. Data center = centroid of all available nonzero CT tissue, after excluding edge-connected components if any; no manual center tuning. Use fixed displacement ±64 level-1 pixels along x/y; mirror x. This deliberately does not assert the anatomical umbilicus. For PHerc1203 also evaluate separately the pinned public official center (Git blob 2496c8ebf6dcd8d8d56e71cabc86360916d19c7f) linearly interpolated at native z, converted /2, when acquired. No selection based on prior sense.

Estimator: polar image radial step 1 level-1 voxel, 2048 uniform clockwise image-angle columns; calculate local radial profile NCC between successive angular columns (cyclic last/first). High-pass radial Gaussian sigma 3 voxels; fixed windows of width 64 radial samples with centers from 0.15–0.8 of median tissue ray extent, stride 32; compare shifts -8..8, parabolic subpixel max; full measured mask and texture std >=2 counts, NCC >=0.55, interior best shift (no clipping); exclude ambiguity if separated peak > best-.03. Aggregate equal-angle median local shifts across windows (>=3 valid windows per angle); only report full-circle result if >=95% angles accepted, interpolate missing angular medians only for cyclic gaps <=8 angles; report gap and coverage. Sum signed displacements to estimate pitch. Positive for increasing atan2(y-cy,x-cx) means CW in sense_chain.py; negative means ACW. Also report four disjoint radial-band estimates with same full-angle support rules (>=2 windows per angle), no inference if center dependence. The method is a local ridge-flow proxy, not a direct same-sheet closure proof; report estimator limitations. Exact sign reversal on synthetic mirror; synthetic tests spirals of pitch ±10 (level1 vox), ellipticity 1.0 and 1.25, displacement 12 px, concentric pitch zero. Positive control must recover known ACW for all 5 centers and mirrored sign, magnitude at least 2.5 level1 vox and <=30, mirror agreement within max(2 vox,20%); same threshold, same 5 centers and mirror for targets, require agreement of at least two radial subbands and no confident opposite. Synthetic gates: all nonzero signs and absolute pitch error <=4 level1 vox, ring abs pitch <=2; no retuning on real slices. Any calibration failure -> no target winding verdict even if signs look plausible. Stop at descriptive results; no private images/coordinates. If acquisition or gates fail, publish fail and next task.
