# Q43: synthetic bright-deposit detectability on native public CT

This is a distinct single-energy feasibility test, not a carbon-ink test or a measured chemical lead concentration. L7 found no reliable carbon column-mass feature; Q43 asks whether a visibly brighter deposit could nevertheless be screened. No unread scroll, unknown candidate, or GPU. The real PHerc0841 public W00 fixture is acquired via the existing hash-verified fetch_native.py (28×768×768 uint8). Its profiles have been analyzed before, so this is a development control, not an independent scroll. The new pixel-level injection outcomes have not been measured by this lane.

The original 128×128 cells in rows 0–1 (12 cells) calibrate each score's 99th percentile on the central 96×96 pixels; rows 4–5 (12 cells) are evaluated, leaving rows 2–3 unused. No target annotations; original pixels are non-injected, not certified blank. All scores are computed per cell. Require source array hash as recorded in the prior source inventory. No invented fallback if data acquisition fails.

For each validation cell use a rotated synthetic straight stroke in the central crop, length 72 px, width 18 px, centered at (64,64), orientation cycling [0,45,90,135] by cell index. This represents ~0.67×0.17 mm at 9.366 µm. Gap baseline is median of planes 0:4 and 24:28 per pixel. Original positive digital column mass is sum(max(CT-gap,0)) across planes 4:24. Inject additional total grayscale mass equal to 10% and 30% of that pixel's original positive mass inside the stroke, equally distributed over central planes 13,14,15, rounded once to uint8 and clipped to 255. Measure realized mass addition relative to original mass (including clipping); doses are digital proxies, not material concentrations. No physical forward model of point-spread function, phase retrieval, or attenuation; the bright straight stroke is simpler than real writing.

Scores: (A) raw maximum of planes 4:24, baseline as Q43; (B) max minus Gaussian local mean (sigma 32 pixels, reflect mode); (C) positive column mass minus Gaussian local mean (same sigma and mode). Compute thresholds using calibration cells only, separate per score, fixed 99th percentile using NumPy linear method. At both doses on 12 evaluation cells record per-cell ROC AUC using injected stroke vs rest of central crop, sensitivity above threshold, non-injected off-stroke fraction above threshold, and paired original unmodified background false-alarm fraction. Report medians and extrema, and per-cell values. No multiple tuning.

Continuation gate for the column-mass score C at nominal 30%: at least 10/12 cells retain ≥80% of requested dose; median sensitivity ≥0.50, median original unmodified background flagged fraction ≤0.02, median off-stroke flagged fraction ≤0.02, median sensitivity exceeds locally corrected max B by ≥0.10. At 10% report without adoption threshold. A pass would justify a genuinely independent, real high-Z material calibration, not application to unread scrolls. Failure closes this digital injection version. Even a pass does not prove actual high-Z ink or reading. CPU only, <20 MB source download, one immediate GitHub Actions run with six-minute job timeout, no recurrence. No data/credentials committed; publish only code, summary and receipt.
