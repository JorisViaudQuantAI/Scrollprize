# Adaptive 055 — reciprocal DTW prior with exact ordered projection (fresh cubes)

Frozen after 054, before any acquisition or labels of the new cubes. Hypothesis: adjacent waveform DTW improves identity but can collapse sheets; the globally ordered cut prevents collapse but its fixed seed template loses identity. Combine them, and separate the effect of geometric projection from the effect of adding CT evidence. No optimization on 054 test scores, no target-label tuning. This is a new comparison, not a claim of a new published algorithm.

Data: public manually annotated PHercParis4 harmonized instance cubes from the same 053 source listing, fixed fresh cubes 07376_03024_04048 and 10448_02512_02512; neither was used in 053 or 054. Physically nonoverlapping with previous crops and each other; same scroll, not independent scrolls. Identical 96^3 center crop, normalization and byte/source accounting. Use exact fixed 054 CT-driven orientation, six sections per cube, seed line only, and common eligible pair evaluation. No replacement. Require >=2 evaluable sections and >=500 eligible pairs per cube. No GT foreground supplied. 120 MiB cap, 6-minute CPU run.

Arms P, I, J, W exactly from 054 unchanged. Compute W once, and for each neighboring trace transition also its reverse DTW correspondence. For a tracked depth q in source column, forward maps it to p, reverse maps p to q'; accumulate |q'-q| along the path separately left/right of anchor. This is an inconsistency proxy, not a calibrated confidence. The primary hybrid H uses fixed original integer unary cost C plus a capped distance prior to W: round(150 * min(|d-W|,12)/(1+cumulative_roundtrip_error/4)). Exact ordered cut, LAM100, slope4, min gap2 and seed anchor as in 054. Also projection-only R: cost 1000*|d-W|, lam0, same hard constraints; and fixed-weight hybrid K: C + 150*min(|d-W|,12), same cut. Report all three; K/R are descriptive comparisons, no selecting best after score access. W may hit boundaries and collide, never clipped before scoring; cut domain 4..91 and seed unchanged. Check that reciprocal path equals exact W forward result; all cut energies and constraints checked. Fixed independent synthetic clean shifted-waveform/forced collision tests and equality of zero-error H and K cost; no tuning. Use common score and pooling from 054, report per-section counts and all unavailable sections. Report roundtrip error distribution and fraction of W paths changed by each projection.

Primary gate H on both fresh cubes: all valid constraints; zero gap<2, near2 not worse than W by >.02, correct manual ID not worse than W by >.03, other-ID rate not higher by >.03; plus near2 gain >=.03 over W on at least one cube, and near2 >= J-.02 on both. If this fails, preserve positive 054 and negative 055 without promoting; even pass is only a development gate. Projection and K reported irrespective of H. Reciprocal consistency not evidence of anatomical correctness. No new ink, no GPU, no private coordinates, no external submission. Use source reader and solver from pinned previous commits.
