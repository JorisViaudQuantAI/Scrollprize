## Status vocabulary

| Label used here | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Executed, public control | Actual computation on already published material; training exposure and label modality remain explicit. |
| Executed, unread exploration | Actual inference on unread material; a negative result is limited to the sampled surface, recipe and review. |
| Engineering verified | Recorded tests or parity checks validate a particular contract or calculation; they do not certify anatomical identity or text sensitivity. |
| Proposed / not yet measured | A protocol, code path, allocation, budget assumption or experiment without the required completed scientific outcome. |
| Candidate, unconfirmed | A visually suggestive output with no independently established letter or reading. |
| Failed advancement | The tested variant missed its stated acceptance rule. Preserve the result; do not silently replace the gate or promote another diagnostic arm. |

“PASS” must always travel with its endpoint. Solver exactness, numerical convergence, CT parity, retained known-ink pixels, readable letters and new discoveries are different endpoints.

## 5. Evidence-backed routing rules

These are the minimal consequences supported by the reviewed results, not newly tuned cutoffs.

| Evidence | Appropriate action | Inference the evidence does not support |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Missing CT/source footprint, invalid geometry or lost result | `unassessable` / repair / reacquire; preserve the attempted-unit denominator | “No ink” or completed negative screening |
| Strong shear/stretch, failed flattening or mismatched positive recipe | Repair geometry or retain a low-sensitivity status; include a same-configuration positive | Promoting an old negative after an unrelated winner test |
| Low detector score or no line proposal | Lower priority within a budget, retain exploration and review opportunities | Permanent exclusion of a scroll or sheet |
| High m7 after snap or a good local correlation | Record local support/proximity and source provenance | Correct winding sense, global sheet identity or depth correctness |
| A single dominant order or two agreeing model families | Follow-up with complete images and structure alternatives | A letter, a physical side, or independent confirmation |
| Good AP but poor fixed-threshold recall | Retain both ranking and operating-point metrics | Detection safety from AP alone |
| A pleasing seam-free PNG | Keep float arrays, masks and the exact reconstruction recipe | Quantitative fidelity from a display export |
| Shared coarse 3D cells | Count comparable workload occupancy | Unique papyrus area or same-sheet duplication |
| A correct solver and passing synthetic suite | Reuse the numerical implementation | Successful anatomical reconstruction |
| A failed advancement gate | Preserve the closed variant and its exact scope | No signal anywhere, or permission to substitute a favourable post-hoc arm |

The final unread statuses should continue to distinguish **uninformative**, **screened under this exact pipeline with no qualifying reading identified**, and **candidate under review**. Add explicit `not_run`, `partial`, `failed_execution` and `review_pending` states at intermediate stages. Do not map them all to negative.

## 6. Avoiding duplicate work and overstated novelty

1. **Reuse the ledger and integration indexes.** The purpose of this file is to connect outcomes and consumers, not create another incompatible experiment numbering system. `chatgpt-032` historically names two different lane reports; use an immutable path/revision, not a bare note number.
2. **Reuse frame and source-support work.** Half-pixel alignment, fixed depth-list conventions, original-triangle support, native readers and exact m7 arithmetic already have results. The new 0800 replay adds carrier-specific verification; it does not invent the half-pixel correction.
3. **Do not restart closed parameter families without a new hypothesis/control.** Recentring, five-scale averaging, proxy wavelet promotion, blind pitch confidence, tested CT corrections and several flatten/normal variants already have explicit failures. A materially new donor or independent endpoint should justify any repetition.
4. **Preserve the known-positive distinction.** The public Nu, 0211 automatic-fit recovery and published 0306B/0343 controls are valuable calibration. They cannot count as new discoveries or independent specimens each time their crop, order or perturbation changes.
5. **Do not count source preparation as an experiment.** The NIST surface-sampling phantom is a protocol in the reviewed index; the anchor-corruption 070 path contains a frozen protocol without a completed result in these snapshots. Upstream FP16 PR drafts and a Progress Prize dossier are artifacts, not evidence that a submission was sent or accepted.
6. **Prioritize the missing native bridge.** In the reviewed 073 record, the full actual 0211 fit-band coarse/fine map set is still requested, not verified available. An exporter with source/geometry/recipe hashes, float maps, layer lists, support and measured cost is a concrete prerequisite for the promised ranking/cost experiment. New archived exploratory maps can test that contract, while their candidate labels remain unconfirmed.

## Publication and interpretation boundary

The new visual package belongs with the reproducible evidence, including its unsuccessful interpretations and context checks. It should be cited as an exploratory investigation, not a discovery announcement. For the industrial funnel, this catalogue supports prioritization, reliable receipts and calibrated review; **no reviewed result yet demonstrates an end-to-end reduction in GPU cost while preserving discovery sensitivity across all eligible scrolls**.
