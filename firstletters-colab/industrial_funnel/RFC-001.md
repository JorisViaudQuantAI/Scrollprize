# RFC-001 — Industrial, coverage-aware First Letters search funnel

**Status:** proposed for cross-agent review; not accepted, not deployed. 2026-11-24. Scope: all 19 currently registered eligible scrolls, with a replaceable dated official eligibility snapshot. Base: Adaptive 071 commit ee94c5df935def0b565670b6e987b5b844b89350. Author: ChatGPT lane. Review requested from Claude, Gemini, Codex, owner. No main merge, organizer submission, unattended compute, or new unread-location disclosure.

## 1. Decision and objectives

Optimize expected *new, independently reviewable, physically coherent evidence* per constrained GPU/CPU/human hour, not rendered area, maximum ink score, or number of runs. A search unit is a physical sheet hypothesis and a registered chart footprint, not a winding number or detector tile. Ten-letter/area rules must be versioned from the current organizer rules before any submission. Known winners are controls only. Do not claim a probability of writing or success until calibration and population support exist.

Three distinct variables:
- material/ink presence (latent, often unknowable);
- observability of the correct sheet at sufficient physical resolution (geometry and acquisition);
- sensitivity of this detector/recipe for that observable text (conditional on geometry, orientation, scale, ink, and training exposure).

A low value of any proxy is not proof of blank papyrus. **No automatic permanent rejection.** Hard gates govern whether a result is admissible as evidence, not whether papyrus deserves future exploration. Keep separate registers for unknown, unmeshed, repair, supported, coarsely screened, ranked, deeply screened, independently reviewed, and deferred. A negative verdict is scoped to a precise physical footprint, recipe and time. Reopen when geometry, detector, resolution, control validity or reviewer evidence changes.

## 3. Data plane and state machine

Private, versioned inventory → source acquisition → topology and metric chart construction → matched positive/negative control → coarse both-order maps → multiple rank lists → budget allocation → deep detector and local geometry repair (separate recipes) → independent physical and visual review → owner decision. Every completed stage creates an immutable receipt; every failed/partial stage creates a failure receipt and a requeue/repair reason. Stage outputs are not automatically accepted scientific results. Human-readable status must never turn "blocked" or "unsupported" into "negative".

One job per immutable fingerprint (scroll+frame+chart+stage+recipe+input hashes+parent job keys+calibration key). Dependency DAG, immutable inputs, content-addressed artifacts. Byte hash + shape/dtype/units/axis/pixel-center, normal sign and slice order (exact list, not "asc" alone), expected footprint/support, acquisition missingness, per-face correspondence, actual model checkpoint and code revision, preprocessing/scale/stride/tiling/halo/blending/offset/precision, configuration, random seed, resource class and cost. Validate at read and write. Distinguish CT acquired, CT stored, tissue present, projected sheet, chart valid, detector output available, and reviewer visibility. Every map accompanies masks; unavailable pixels never zero-valued evidence. Public versioned model licenses/source rights.

**Coverage:** retain stable original-face/physical-UV IDs and partial polygons, frame transform and uncertainty, native metric area, side/order and depth recipe. Four independent denominators: scan availability; reconstructed unique physical area (only certified identity unions); full-stack-supported and actually inferred unique chart area; independently reviewed physical area. Report overlap lower/upper bounds if identity unresolved, not optimistic union. Partition chart into nonoverlapping 10 mm accounting cores, infer padded/overlapping windows with halo sufficient for context, expand to 20 mm review regions, clip to valid support. Store both nominal and supported cost, merge only certified same-sheet footprints, never use nearest points alone. Cross-band seams, duplicated/reversed wraps and recto/verso are not independent area. If total material area unknown, fraction null. Publish per-scroll aggregate coverage, not chart IDs, positions or candidate images.

## 4. Ranking and budget allocation

Use a *portfolio* of independent-ish cues: high detector scores (quantiles and mass), local line chains, dense-row spectral/periodic coherence, morphology/stroke persistence, detector/order/depth agreement, uncertainty/geometry quality, and spatial diversity. Treat correlations between detectors sharing training data as dependencies. Rank each recipe/scroll internally; no comparison of raw p99 across scrolls. A shared calibration stratum requires evidence (source resolution, ink regime, grid distortions, model exposure). Q34, bilateral asymmetry and crack filters are diagnostics: do not use as sole admission gates. Keep max and individual offsets rather than only mean; a single weak line can be valid. OCR/glyph language-model priors only for human review, never self-generated letters as ground truth.

For a mature calibrated stratum, maximize expected incremental value per measured cost under resource vector constraints (GPU time, CPU time, I/O, human time, memory/thermal capacity), with shared evidence dedup and per-scroll floors. Before calibration, use ordinal ranks and deterministic interleaving, not invented probabilities. Initial proposed resource budgets per allocation epoch: 70% exploitation, 20% repair/novel geometry, 10% independent exploration. Configurable by owner, frozen per epoch, no automatic borrowing; a resource infeasible for one lane is reported, not silently spent elsewhere. Each scroll must receive a registration/geometry opportunity; progress floors across eligible scrolls, not starvation by cheap dense atlas. Track queue age and cost; report the bottleneck when a floor cannot be funded. The prototype supports lane-specific integer vector caps and least-served scroll selection; cost-optimal portfolio and global progress floors require production adapter.

**Exploration is a survey, not a token random choice after ranking.** Freeze eligible physical accounting units and strata BEFORE observing fine outcomes: scroll × quality/geometry × rank band × acquisition/ink regime, with unmeshed regions in a separate geometry survey. Draw uniform without replacement inside each stratum; store seed, population hash, sampled membership privately, N/n and inclusion probability. Preallocate resource budget for draw; if costs exceed reserve, declare incomplete, do not substitute favorable units. Use the same independent reference adjudication for high/low rank. Estimate missed *detectable positives under the deep-review recipe*, not all latent ink. Horvitz–Thompson estimates with design uncertainty; exact finite-population zero-event upper bound only when every sampled unit adjudicated, with simultaneous bounds across strata (Bonferroni). If missing labels, review failure or incomplete sampling, no upper bound. Never generalize a known-positive crop's retention to all 19 scrolls. Reserve new anchors/poor quality survey and entirely unmeshed discovery; sampling bias in current chart pool is distinct from rank bias.

## 6. Control plane and safety

Proposal: single private coordinator initially (SQLite WAL, one host, worker leases via an authenticated API later). No SQLite on shared network filesystem. Workers receive exact immutable jobs and bounded grants. Private artifact store CAS, verify hashes on receipt; producer/worker validation attestations are not scientific truth. State transitions transactionally journaled: PENDING→LEASED→SUCCEEDED or RETRY/BLOCKED; expiry fenced, charge reserved budget on lost attempt; stale worker cannot publish a result. Same completion receipt replay idempotent, different receipt conflict. New recipe = new job, prior negative immutable. No uncontrolled external command execution in reference implementation. Per-epoch caps and lane balances, explicit resource request with p95 estimates and margin. Worker must enforce actual resource/time/thermal limits and stop on expiry; database reservation alone cannot kill an external worker. Overrun charged and circuit breaker; operator must reconcile expired jobs before retries in real deployment. Idempotent upload, content hash validation, crash/restart and backups, role-based auth, secrets isolation, TLS, audit retention, offline restore test, versioned schema migrations. Only synthetic/public-control CI. Multi-host deployment, authenticated leases, secrets, active revocation of gates and physical coverage union are open production work, not claims of this prototype.

Public progress: per-scroll aggregate counts of registered/support-blocked/geometry repair/coarse/deep/review/exploration, unique area bounds + denominator status, budget reservation/actual/wasted, error reasons and calibration status. Never public chart IDs or hashes that deanonymize a private candidate, source paths/coordinates/maps, or per-candidate timestamps. A candidate statement only in agreed collab wording. Private audit logs encrypted and access limited; retention policy chosen by owner; no auto-delete evidence. Observability separate resource and science metrics: throughput, p50/p95 duration, failure/retry count, duplicate saved cost, queue age, memory/OOM, bytes, per-stage attrition, recall and worst stratum, false-safe reviewed misses.

## 7. Rollout and measurable promotion

**R0 (this RFC):** cross-agent review, executable control-plane and survey contracts on synthetic fixtures; actual prior 071 evidence included. No deployment authority.
**R1 public adapter integration:** Claude/Gemini export exact native both-order coarse and deep maps + masks in identical chart frame, source/recipe fingerprints and measured cost, on 0211/0306B/0343 and at least one genuinely withheld manuscript/control family. Include blank margins, folds, sparse single line, opposite side, displaced ±one/two local pitches, corrupt anchor, high shear, different scan resolution, unobserved chunks, near seam; truth kept outside ranking, record training exposure. Codex verifies known controls and negative perturbations independently. No winner-crop calibration alone.
**R2 frozen sensitivity/cost benchmark:** compare full deep evaluation vs funnel under fixed cost at 25/50/75/100% + exploration. Same valid common footprint and complete end-to-end costs (render/transfer/flatten/coarse/rank/deep/review). Primary endpoints: discovery of source-known text regions after independent review (not probability or box alone), supported row/letter coverage, per-positive survival through every stage, worst scroll/stratum, missed detectable positives in survey, cost per verified region, p95 resource/latency. Proposed conservative acceptance: all mandatory known positive text units reachable, no silent missingness, >=95% measured supported target survival AND no worse than 5pp vs full-deep on each supported positive stratum, no wrong-sheet promotion; at least 20% measured total resource reduction in the constrained bottleneck with matched coverage and noninferiority. Require independent physical scroll holdout and report clustered confidence intervals; if insufficient sample, no statistically established noninferiority and no irreversible exclusion. Freeze final gates with agents before data. Fault-injection suite (killed worker, duplicate, stale token, changed input, corrupt payload, OOM/timeout, quota exhausted, gate failed, incomplete survey) all pass. Any physical failure -> no production promotion even if CI green.
**R3 bounded pilot:** owner approves budget, hardware and private data access. Select a small cross-scroll tranche with known-good chart quality + repair + probability sample; every scroll tracked in registry. Human-reviewed results and actual costs private. Publish sanitized aggregate only; don't open claim without permission. Sequential expansion only after recall and resource gates hold on new cohorts. Do not launch blanket scans or recurring jobs from this RFC.
