# B11b: exact minimum budget across all fixed stratified allocations

Follow-up after first B11 run. For each fixed target M in {6,12} and conservative count U in {0,1,2,3}, use the B11 SRS minimum as an information-theoretic lower bound: if the M flagged units were drawn uniformly from the N population, the distribution of sampled flag count for ANY independent fixed-size sample design of total size b is Hypergeom(N,M,b). Therefore the maximum over fixed allocations of flags is at least this average. No stratified design can reject at a budget smaller than the SRS minimum. The B11 balanced nested path supplies a feasible upper bound, differing by at most one draw.

Enumerate every feasible positive integer allocation at the SRS bound through the balanced bound. Prune a design only if one explicit concentrated flag allocation m=M in one stratum violates the exact p<1/20 rule. For survivors calculate exact worst CDF by enumeration, retain number feasible, choose lexicographically first feasible design (not a data-dependent outcome choice). Do not imply such a plan is best for physical chart touch, real costs or ink labels. Same fixed prospective sampling assumptions. Source and test code are the already published B11 counts, not fresh review outcomes. This is an optimization of the design, not a new inference or optional stopping rule.
