# PHerc. Paris 4 — pre-read native Zarr I/O feasibility

This is a **metadata-only CPU planning experiment**, not an ink detector, registration result, or observed network measurement. It tests one falsifiable assumption in the proposed Paris 4 78/137 keV preflight before opening unread CT pixels. It complements the GPU First Letters screeners and Codex's CT geometry work without launching competing inference.

## Result, 2026-11-22

The frozen question (`frozen_protocol.json`, SHA-256 `16c2286dd480d90f8848132832caf86f969ef93ecb46c75737619ff5580864ff`) is whether **three independent paired 2×2 mm native L0 scan controls** can be read under a permissive **180 MiB total raw-network budget** from the **uncompressed whole-object Zarr v2 chunks**, even with one plane in depth, ideal alignment and zero halo. No CT chunks have been retrieved. After freezing settings, the two official L0 `.zarray` metadata files were read from public S3 and snapshotted here with their original field values; both are uint8 C-order with 128×128×128 uncompressed 2 MiB chunks.

A 2 mm span contains ceil(2000/2.4) = 834 pixels. In one scan, even a perfectly aligned 834×834×1 interior support touches ceil(834/128)^2 = 49 chunks, **98 MiB** of full chunk payload. The same support in both scans takes **196 MiB** before alignment halo, request overhead or extra depths. Three disjoint, non-reusing pairs take **588 MiB best-case**. Arbitrary one-voxel interior XY phase can require 8×8 chunks per scan (**256 MiB one pair**). The largest square per pair that three disjoint pairs could fit in the 180 MiB whole-chunk ceiling is 384×384 pixels, or **921.6 µm**, before halos. This is too small to substitute uncritically for the frozen 2 mm geometry supports.

**Decision:** Under the specified direct whole-chunk fetching path (the current streamed Zarr reader), the planned 2 mm paired Step 0 exceeds the 180 MiB budget even for **one** pair; do not launch that plan on unread data as-is. This does **not** show that 2 mm paired controls are scientifically impossible: change the retrieval path or increase the budget, then re-freeze the complete plan before looking at a residual. Candidate alternatives need verification: sparse HTTP byte-range reads on raw uncompressed chunks (with independently checked server Range support and row/Z byte layout), pre-existing pre-cropped/level-downsampled products, or coarser physical-resolution controls with explicitly reconciled transforms and PSFs. Never substitute absent chunks or masked zeros for acquisition validity.

The bound is conditional on a wholly present source-supported patch, axis-aligned scanner-native square, separate single-voxel Z slices and whole-object GETs. A surface-following oblique patch, affine uncertainty, layer normals, scanner FOV, or 3D alignment may increase demand or change chunk reuse; none were measured. HTTP gzip / partial range, cloud cache sharing, compressed and rechunked stores are **outside** this model. Even the per-scan paired fields' physical intersection is not established by metadata dimensions; do not select controls by guessed coordinate overlap.
