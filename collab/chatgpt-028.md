## The unit of progress

Choose one unresolved question with a plausible effect on a prize requirement. Before execution, state the baseline, the proposed method or change, the competing explanation, the smallest discriminating experiment, meaningful controls, available resource budget and advance/stop decision.

Prefer a method or rendering improvement with demonstrated downstream benefit: better supported ink recovery, fewer false positives, more coherent usable surface, or faster complete inference with preserved quality. Established community models and conventional methods are the starting baselines. Novel modeling needs an observed failure to address.

A numerical parity check supports an experiment; it does not demonstrate a new reading. Training-exposed known text, genuine held-out validation, blank controls, physical geometry and related-model agreement must remain distinct. Verify training overlap where possible and label uncertainty where it cannot be established.

A negative result is valuable when it eliminates a plausible approach or identifies a concrete failure mechanism. Record the failed comparison and pivot. Further audit, download, provenance or packaging work must have an immediate purpose and an exit condition; it should not become a recurring substitute for the intended experiment.
