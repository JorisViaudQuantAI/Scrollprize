# Frozen extension of the public 24-site PHerc. 0813 audit

This extension reuses the 24 ordered sites selected in the original protocol in commit `898b5052f40bd711fb6732861b6cd8b05a9e2bc8`. The winding, sample eligibility, original normals, five-ray stencil, CT thresholds, tie tolerance and endpoint stay fixed. The published aggregate results were already known. All 24 IDs will be reported. For a later no-tuning rendering comparison, even IDs are reserved and odd IDs are available for pipeline checks. That split does not create a previously unseen CT cohort.

Let P and S be the raw and snapped XYZ at a given public UV. Normalize the released finite-difference normals before measuring their angle. Measure the physical distance P→S and split that displacement along and across the original normal. For an offset of 6, 12 and 24 native voxels report the separation caused solely by the change in normal at the same physical center.

The operational anatomical reference is A = P + t Nraw, where t is the unique bright crest nearest zero on the frozen raw CT profile. It is a point on the central sampling axis derived from an average of five rays. Equidistant raw peaks stay ambiguous. A maximum can arise from a fibre, another interface or the same sheet. There is one native acquisition and both m7 and the CT profiles derive from it.

Project P, and A where unique, onto the snapped triangle mesh by closest Euclidean distance. The fixed diagonal runs from the upper left to the lower right of each valid UV quad. Preserve the triangle, barycentric weights, interpolated chart position and distinct projection ties. At P, S and the projected P, report the signed depth and transverse miss of that same 3D landmark A in the local normal frame. Report separately the CT peaks on rays through P with the raw and snapped normal, through S with the snapped normal, and through projected P with the interpolated normal. Retain the original search ±24 voxels, measure zero-peak and distance ties, distinguish a nearest peak from the fixed anatomical reference and report the 3D distance to the nearest candidate crest for each ray. Use the original acquisition units, 9.362 µm per native voxel. All selection uses the public atlas geometry and all derived fields describe geometry or native CT profiles.
